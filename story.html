When the phone rang at 3 a.m.: a decade of rebuilding

Priya Nandan · Oct 12, 2026

I used to think survival was a finish line — a place you crossed and then stood, breathless and safe, on the other side. Ten years after the night that changed everything, I understand now that survival is a practice. It is the small, unglamorous decision to continue, made again and again in the spaces no one else sees.

This is the story of how I learned that healing is not linear, and how the cracks in the places I broke have slowly become places where something new can grow.
